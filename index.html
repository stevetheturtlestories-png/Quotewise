FAQ

Questions homeowners are likely to ask.

Does ChoiceGrade tell me which contractor to hire?

No. It helps you compare the information you have, highlights differences and missing details, and helps you ask better questions.

Can I compare more than three quotes?

Yes. ChoiceGrade supports two to five contractors in one comparison.

Is this only for HVAC?

No. ChoiceGrade supports HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, renovations, windows and doors, landscaping, pools/spas/hot tubs and general repairs.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. ChoiceGrade is designed as an installable web app so it can live on your home screen and open like an app.
